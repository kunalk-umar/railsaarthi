60-Day Advance Reservation Period (ARP)

The advance reservation window was officially cut from 120 days to 60 days (excluding journey day) to curb bulk block-booking, reduce ticket hoarding, and prevent cancellations.

✓ Shorter reservation periods remain for select intercity express day trains.
Effective 5 Jan 2026 verified_user

Aadhaar Priority Booking Window

On the day general bookings open (Day 60), Aadhaar-authenticated IRCTC users receive an exclusive window from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. General unlinked users can only book after 4:00 PM.

✓ Link your Aadhaar under IRCTC Profile → Authenticate Aadhaar.
Standing + Jul 2025 timer

Tatkal Schedule & Mandatory OTP

Tatkal bookings open 1 day prior to departure (excluding journey date):
• AC Classes (1A/2A/3A/CC/EC): Opens at 10:00 AM
• Non-AC Classes (SL/2S): Opens at 11:00 AM
Since July 2025, mandatory Aadhaar identity + OTP validation is enforced across IRCTC website, PRS reservation counters, and authorized agents.

✓ Max 4 passengers per Tatkal PNR ticket.
Directive Jul 2025 assignment_turned_in

8-Hour Reservation Chart Preparation

First reservation charts are now prepared 8 hours prior to train departure (shifted from 4 hours previously), giving passengers ample advance notice on RAC confirmation and berth allocation.

✓ Current booking window remains open until 30 minutes before departure for vacant berths.
Tariff Notification price_change

Official Fare Revision (26 Dec 2025)

• Ordinary Non-AC: +1 paisa per km (Sleeper & 1st Ordinary)
• Mail/Express (Non-AC & AC): +2 paise per km
• Suburban Daily & Season Tickets: Zero hike (unchanged)
This follows a marginal hike in July 2025 to fund safety and passenger amenities.

Anti-Fraud Drive shield

Anti-Fraud & Tout Elimination

Indian Railways and RPF have removed over 3 crore suspicious ticketing accounts and arrested 13,580 illegal touts between 2023 and June 2026. Automated bot detection software safeguards opening-minute bookings.
